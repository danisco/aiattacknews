Agentic threat activity crosses a significant threshold

The newest AI attack news is a step change in autonomy.           About AI Attack News · Read more
          

            
AI Attack News is the news page of the AI Attack Tracker. If you searched for AI attack news, AI attack news today or latest AI attack news, this is the page that pulls the stories together in one place, newest first, with what happened, who found it, who was targeted and how serious the tracker grades it.

            
Every story here comes from a published report by the company or agency that found the attack. AI Attack News summarises that report, links you to the full original reporting, and links you back to the matching record in the AI Attack Tracker and on the AI Attack Map. No rumours, no unnamed claims and no story without a source report.

            
AI Attack News at aiattacknews.com collects the latest AI attack news on autonomous AI attacks, agentic AI attacks, AI assisted intrusions, AI in malware, AI powered credential harvesting and AI orchestrated espionage. It sits alongside the AI Attack Tracker and the AI Attack Map so a reader who arrives for AI attack news today can move straight to the evidence: the tracker record, the severity and evidence grade, the map pin where a location is documented, and the original published report. When a new verified AI attack report is published, it belongs in AI Attack News, in the tracker feed and on the map together.

            
Dates on this page are publication dates. Where a report gives a different activity date or a publication delay, the tracker record shows both, and where a source gives no activity date the record says not dated in report rather than guessing.
