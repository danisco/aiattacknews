Anthropic reports AI-assisted espionage attempts against roughly 30 targets

Anthropic says attackers used Claude Code against technology companies, financial institutions, manufacturers and government agencies. A small number of attempts succeeded.

AI Attack News · Published November 23, 2026

What Anthropic observed

The company detected unusual activity in September 2025. Its investigation found that the operator directed an AI agent to perform substantial portions of the work. Anthropic assessed the group as Chinese state-sponsored with high confidence.

What the target count means

About 30 organizations were targeted. Anthropic distinguished that figure from the smaller number of confirmed intrusions. It said it disabled associated accounts, notified affected parties where appropriate and coordinated with authorities.
