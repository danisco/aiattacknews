Amazon says AI agents found a new flaw in medical imaging software

Cairn agents took control of a server during a campaign targeting East Asian universities. The operator chose the targets, and the agents worked out how to proceed.

AI Attack News · Published November 23, 2026

What the activity records show

Amazon reconstructed the September 8–10 campaign from records left on the operator’s server. Two efforts to gain access failed, including one that lasted roughly 16 hours. On a server running MONAI Label, the agents found a previously undocumented flaw and later gained full control, Amazon reported on November 20.

The records also show where a human intervened. The operator subsequently directed the agents into another university network. Amazon inferred how the operator gained that foothold. Its account does not establish whether anyone obtained patient or research data.

What maintainers have fixed

The MONAI Label maintainers published a critical advisory on November 18. It identifies versions through 0.8.5 as affected and links the correction in the source code. As of November 23, the advisory names no patched package version. That distinction matters to teams deciding whether the software they installed includes the fix.
