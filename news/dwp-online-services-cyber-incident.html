UK takes benefits services offline after cyber incident, says payments will continue

The Department for Work and Pensions says regular benefit and pension payments are on schedule. Some online services, including the Pension Credit calculator, are unavailable.

AI Attack News · Published November 27, 2026

The UK Department for Work and Pensions said on November 27 that it had taken some online services offline after detecting a cyber incident. People due to receive regular benefit or pension payments should still receive them as scheduled, the department said.

Which services are affected?

The Guardian reported problems with online claims for Universal Credit and Personal Independence Payment, the Pension Credit calculator and Tell Us Once, which families can use to notify government departments of a death. DWP advised anyone unable to use an online service to try again later.

The department also warned people about messages seeking bank details or asking them to follow a link. It said it would never contact them that way.

What investigators have said

DWP described the shutdown as a precaution. It is investigating with the National Cyber Security Centre and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre. Its notice gave no timetable for full restoration and did not identify the cause, say that claimant data had been accessed or attribute any role to AI.
