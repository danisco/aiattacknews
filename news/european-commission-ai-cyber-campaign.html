European Commission confirms AI tools were used in cyber campaign

The Commission temporarily took applications offline after detecting the campaign on October 22. Most had returned to service by its November 19 briefing.

AI Attack News · Published November 23, 2026

What the Commission disclosed

Spokesperson Thomas Regnier said investigators confirmed the incident on October 26. The Commission contacted CERT-EU and the European Data Protection Supervisor. At the briefing, it acknowledged that a publicly available AI tool was involved.

What investigators left open

Officials declined to identify the attacker or estimate the full scale. The public briefing establishes AI use but does not identify the tool or establish how much work it carried out independently.
