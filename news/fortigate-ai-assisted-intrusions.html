Amazon traces AI-assisted intrusions to more than 600 FortiGate devices

Amazon observed compromises across more than 55 countries between January 11 and February 18. The investigation points to weak access controls.

AI Attack News · Published November 23, 2026

What connected the incidents

Amazon described a financially motivated operator using commercial AI services to extend the reach of familiar techniques. Its investigation found exposed management interfaces and accounts protected by only one authentication factor.

Why the distinction matters

The number of devices affected could suggest a new flaw in the product. Amazon reported no evidence of a FortiGate vulnerability being exploited in this campaign. Its findings put the focus on who could reach administrative interfaces and how those users authenticated.
