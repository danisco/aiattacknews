Google reports a credential-theft campaign assembled in under six hours

Google says attackers used AI agents after gaining access to a cloud resource in the second quarter of 2026.

AI Attack News · Published November 23, 2026

Where the clock starts

Researchers described a period of less than six hours to plan, build and run a mass credential-harvesting operation. That window began after the attackers had already compromised a cloud resource. It does not measure the time taken to obtain initial access.

What else Google saw

The September report also describes attempts to steal AI assets and use other organizations’ cloud accounts to run workloads. For victims, the exposure can extend beyond stolen information to the cost of compute consumed on their accounts.
