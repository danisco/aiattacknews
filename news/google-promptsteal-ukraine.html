Google documents AI-assisted malware in attacks on Ukraine

Google linked PROMPTSTEAL to Russian group APT28. A second malware family described in the same report was still experimental.

AI Attack News · Published November 23, 2026

Two different findings

Google said it identified PROMPTSTEAL in activity against Ukraine in June. The malware used a language model while operating. Ukraine’s computer emergency response team had reported the same malware under the name LAMEHUG.

Why the second sample matters

Researchers also examined PROMPTFLUX. Parts of that sample were incomplete, and Google said it had seen no demonstrated ability to compromise a device or network. The evidence for deployment differs for the two families.
