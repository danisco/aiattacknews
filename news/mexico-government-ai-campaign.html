Gambit links two AI platforms to a campaign targeting Mexican agencies

Gambit Security says one operator used Claude Code and GPT-4.1 in a campaign spanning late December 2025 to mid-February 2026.

AI Attack News · Published November 23, 2026

What the records show

The researchers say they recovered 1,088 prompts across 34 sessions. They attribute roughly three quarters of remote command execution to Claude Code. That estimate concerns the commands they reconstructed, rather than the share of every decision made during the campaign.

How the account developed

Gambit disclosed initial findings in February and published its fuller technical account on April 10 after giving affected parties time to respond. Its estimate of nine affected organizations is the investigative team’s assessment.
