Osaki Electric plans to restart smart-meter production after ransomware attack

The Japanese manufacturer expects production to resume in stages from November 27. Customers with orders already placed face a longer wait for shipments.

AI Attack News · Published November 25, 2026

Osaki Electric says it can begin making smart meters again from November 27 at three Japanese production sites where work stopped after a ransomware attack. The company confirmed the attack on November 17 and suspended production and shipments to contain it.

When can customers expect shipments?

In its November 25 notice, Osaki said it expects to start shipping existing orders from December 1, as individual products clear checks. It will give customers their own delivery dates. Normal processing of new orders is expected to resume in stages from December 7 as Osaki checks connections with customer systems.

Osaki said the production equipment itself escaped damage from the attack, and it had found no effect on products or services already in use. Separately, investigators established that data left internal systems and that someone gained unauthorized access to group company Osaki Electric Systems. They are still determining which data left and how much. Osaki identified ransomware and supplied no evidence of AI involvement.
