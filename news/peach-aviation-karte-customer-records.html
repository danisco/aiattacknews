Peach Aviation says up to 9.44 million customer records leaked from former vendor

The airline stopped using KARTE in 2025. Customer names, addresses and contact details remained in the system when an intruder accessed it this November.

AI Attack News · Published November 26, 2026

Peach Aviation said on November 26 that up to 9.44 million customer records leaked from KARTE, a marketing service the Japanese airline stopped using in August 2025. The figure counts records and may include multiple entries for the same person. Peach said the leaked information came from around 2019 to 2023.

Why a former vendor still had the records

Peach used KARTE to send information to customers through its website. The airline said its contract required PLAID, the service provider, to delete the data when the relationship ended. According to Peach, the vendor acknowledged that it had retained records in error. Peach has asked for written confirmation that PLAID deletes all remaining copies, including backups and logs.

PLAID detected unauthorized access on November 13 and confirmed the leak on November 17. The incident occurred on the vendor’s systems. Neither company’s disclosure identifies AI involvement.

What customers can expect

The records included names, birth dates, postal and email addresses, and phone numbers. Peach said the exposed data excluded passport and payment card details. It plans to notify affected customers it can identify and has reported the incident to Japan’s Personal Information Protection Commission.

Peach said it had seen no misuse by the time of its notice. It warned customers to be cautious about calls or messages claiming to come from the airline or PLAID, particularly if they contain links or request a callback.
