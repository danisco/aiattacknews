Rackspace gives Spot customers until December 8 to move their data

Rackspace says it will shut down the cloud service at 5 p.m. Central after a breach exposed customer systems. Applications still running on Spot will stop at the deadline.

AI Attack News · Published November 25, 2026

Customers running applications on Rackspace Spot have until December 8 to move them elsewhere and retrieve their data. Rackspace set the deadline in a customer notice obtained by BleepingComputer and reported November 24. It applies to Spot, the company’s service for running applications in Kubernetes clusters.

What happens at the deadline

Rackspace told customers that it will stop the remaining workloads at 5 p.m. Central and that they will lose access to the data left on the service. For a business hosting an application there, copying its files and getting the application running on another provider are separate jobs to finish before the cutoff.

The closure follows a breach in which intruders gained access to customer clusters and secrets, including credentials used by applications, BleepingComputer reported. The customer notice provided no evidence that the intruders used AI.
