SAP investigates security incident after reports of source code access

SAP said it had found no evidence that anyone accessed customers’ business data. Reports about copied code and possible AI assistance remain unconfirmed by the company.

AI Attack News · Published November 27, 2026

SAP said on November 27 that it had detected suspicious activity and taken steps to protect its customers and services. The German enterprise software company told Reuters that investigators had found no evidence so far of unauthorized access to customers’ business data.

What was reported about the code

German news agency dpa reviewed internal SAP communications that placed the suspicious activity on GitHub, according to SecurityWeek. Der Spiegel, citing an insider, reported that intruders obtained employees’ access credentials and used them to copy and change code in repositories.

SAP has not publicly identified the code involved. It remains unclear whether any proprietary code was accessed, and no evidence has emerged that altered software reached customers. The company’s statement about customer business data leaves those questions open.

What is known about AI involvement?

Sources cited by Der Spiegel suggested AI may have assisted the attackers. SAP did not confirm that account in its public response, and the reports do not establish what an AI system did or identify who was responsible.
