Switzerland reports four break-ins from five AI-assisted attacks

Three critical infrastructure operators were among the targets. The Swiss cybersecurity chief said the attacks caused no major outages.

AI Attack News · Published November 25, 2026 · Updated November 26, 2026

November 26 update · Four attacks succeeded

Attackers using AI agents broke into computer systems in four of five recent attacks in Switzerland, the federal cybersecurity office told Reuters. Three critical infrastructure operators were among the targets. The agency did not say which of them were breached.

Florian Schütz, who leads the agency, told Swiss public broadcaster SRF that there had been no major outages. The attackers and the affected organizations remain unidentified.

Clarification, November 26. Our November 25 report said attackers gained access in every case. That described the cases in the agency’s November 24 bulletin. Its latest count includes one unsuccessful attack.

What the Swiss agency established

In its November 24 bulletin, the centre said the attacks affected organizations across at least two sectors. It did not identify the sectors, the organizations or the number of victims. It confirmed access to their computer systems and gave no indication that power, transport or other public services had stopped.

The centre said AI agents could act on weaknesses much faster than a human operator, leaving defenders less time to intervene. It urged operators to protect internet-facing services with multifactor authentication and to apply critical updates to those systems within 24 hours.

A separate warning from Italy

Italy’s national incident response team reported on November 23 that it had observed campaigns in which multiple AI agents worked in parallel. BleepingComputer reported on both warnings on November 25. Neither agency identified a common attacker.
