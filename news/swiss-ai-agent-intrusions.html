Switzerland confirms AI-assisted intrusions at critical infrastructure operators

The Swiss National Cyber Security Centre says organizations across at least two sectors reported successful intrusions. It has yet to name the operators or identify a public service that was disrupted.

AI Attack News · Published November 25, 2026

Switzerland’s National Cyber Security Centre says it has received its first confirmed reports of attackers using AI agents to break into Swiss organizations. The cases included critical infrastructure operators. In every case reported to the agency, the attackers gained access to the victim’s IT systems.

What the Swiss agency established

In its November 24 bulletin, the centre said the attacks affected organizations across at least two sectors. It did not identify the sectors, the organizations or the number of victims. It confirmed access to their computer systems and gave no indication that power, transport or other public services had stopped.

The centre said AI agents could act on weaknesses much faster than a human operator, leaving defenders less time to intervene. It urged operators to protect internet-facing services with multifactor authentication and to apply critical updates to those systems within 24 hours.

A separate warning from Italy

Italy’s national incident response team reported on November 23 that it had observed campaigns in which multiple AI agents worked in parallel. BleepingComputer reported on both warnings on November 25. Neither agency identified a common attacker.
